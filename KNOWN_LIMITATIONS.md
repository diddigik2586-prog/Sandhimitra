# Known limitations (honest status)
**Verification not possible in the build environment:** no Android device/emulator (no KVM). The APK was built, signed and structurally verified (apksigner, aapt2), and the debug app's screens/navigation were exercised under Robolectric (JVM). **It has not been installed or launched on a real device**; offline/airplane-mode, device restart persistence, Back on hardware, QR scanning on-device and sharing/PDF intents are untested on hardware. Release build uses R8 shrinking and was not run.
**Fonts (v0.1):** Tiro Devanagari Hindi, Noto Sans Kannada and Andika, all SIL OFL 1.1; licence texts ship in the APK (assets/licenses).
**Supplied images:** the Support page now shows the QR cropped (pixels unchanged) from your support image, and the payment details as native text transcribed from it; the QR decodes to the printed UPI ID. The About-DVK image is a screenshot of another app; the About page uses the supplied photograph plus text transcribed from the About image (Sanskrit/Kannada versions are my translations — please review). The wordmark has an opaque white background (no alpha), so it sits on a white plate in dark theme and splash.
**Menu label:** English label "Support Sandhimitra" is typed as listed in the spec; Sanskrit/Kannada labels avoid typing the brand.
**English content:** per-topic overview, sūtras, notes are independent English; derivations, examples and exercises in English mode are Sanskrit source text in IAST; the long Sanskrit/Kannada explanatory paragraphs are not translated. Self-study Q&A has no English translation (English practice uses generated drills + sūtra recall).
**Engine scope:** covers the registry rules only (see Registry screen). Not implemented: padānta conjunct reduction (8.2.23; flagged Provisional), final ś/ṣ/h/l-initial special cases, ṅaḥ kuk-ṭuk śari, 8.3.40/41 visarga→s/ṣ, lexical pragṛhya detection beyond a small list (dual/pragṛhya must be declared by the user), vṛddhi/pararūpa distinction needs root hints (small list). Reverse analysis handles one junction at a time. Verb forms are only recognised heuristically (never "Verified").
**Lexicon:** 194,083 Monier-Williams headwords (stems) + 793 forms attested in the documents + 43 closed-class items. No full inflection tables, no verb paradigms, no corpora. Not "complete" Sanskrit coverage. MW data is CC BY-SA 4.0 (share-alike applies to that data).
**Other:** release keystore password is the default `sandhimitra-release` — generate your own for publication. Room was replaced by plain SQLite to reduce dependencies.

**Sample build 1.0.1-sample:** in-app keyboard and the lexicon page were removed at the author's request (the engine still uses the bundled word list internally to check components; the About page credits it). Practice answers are optional reveal-only. Status label 'Provisional' is displayed as 'Possible' and means the analysis is grammatically valid but a component is only heuristically recognised (e.g. a verb form). Still not hardware-tested.

**Wordmark:** at the author's request the header/splash wordmark is a derived transparent version of the supplied PNG (white background removed, artwork unchanged; original kept in original_assets/images). The header centre is no longer empty and carries the wordmark; the icon sits at the left.

**Corpus check (1.0.5):** 17 forward and 12 reverse items of the supplied tables still differ; they are source-table defects (wrong spellings, truncated cells, rows whose answer belongs to another row, उत्थम्भनम्, गुरवादेशः…) or morphology beyond Sandhi (मृण्मयम्, हंसः). Listed in docs/CORPUS_CHECK_REPORT.txt for the author's review.

**Reverse ranking (1.1.1):** when a joined form has several grammatical readings, the first one shown is chosen from dictionary evidence (entry size, compound markers, citation forms), not from meaning; truly ambiguous forms can still put a valid but less usual reading first. All readings remain available under "Other possible analyses".
