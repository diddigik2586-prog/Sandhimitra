# Known limitations (honest status)
**Verification not possible in the build environment:** no Android device/emulator (no KVM). The APK was built, signed and structurally verified (apksigner, aapt2), and the debug app's screens/navigation were exercised under Robolectric (JVM). **It has not been installed or launched on a real device**; offline/airplane-mode, device restart persistence, Back on hardware, QR scanning on-device and sharing/PDF intents are untested on hardware. Release build uses R8 shrinking and was not run.
**Fonts:** the supplied Kohinoor family is *Latin only* (no Devanagari, Kannada, or IAST dot-below letters) and its licence is "Personal Use Only" (Befonts) — confirm redistribution rights before publishing. Kohinoor is used for all Latin text; Noto Sans Devanagari/Kannada/Noto Sans (OFL) cover the rest.
**Supplied images:** the Support image is a screenshot from another app and prints "Support Śodhamitra" and the title bar of that app; the About-DVK image is likewise a screenshot headed "Śabdamitra". Per the spec the Support image is shown unmodified; the About page uses the supplied photograph plus text transcribed from the About image (Sanskrit/Kannada versions are my translations — please review). The wordmark has an opaque white background (no alpha), so it sits on a white plate in dark theme and splash.
**Menu label:** English label "Support Sandhimitra" is typed as listed in the spec; Sanskrit/Kannada labels avoid typing the brand.
**English content:** per-topic overview, sūtras, notes are independent English; derivations, examples and exercises in English mode are Sanskrit source text in IAST; the long Sanskrit/Kannada explanatory paragraphs are not translated. Self-study Q&A has no English translation (English practice uses generated drills + sūtra recall).
**Engine scope:** covers the registry rules only (see Registry screen). Not implemented: padānta conjunct reduction (8.2.23; flagged Provisional), final ś/ṣ/h/l-initial special cases, ṅaḥ kuk-ṭuk śari, 8.3.40/41 visarga→s/ṣ, lexical pragṛhya detection beyond a small list (dual/pragṛhya must be declared by the user), vṛddhi/pararūpa distinction needs root hints (small list). Reverse analysis handles one junction at a time. Verb forms are only recognised heuristically (never "Verified").
**Lexicon:** 194,083 Monier-Williams headwords (stems) + 793 forms attested in the documents + 43 closed-class items. No full inflection tables, no verb paradigms, no corpora. Not "complete" Sanskrit coverage. MW data is CC BY-SA 4.0 (share-alike applies to that data).
**Other:** release keystore password is the default `sandhimitra-release` — generate your own for publication. Room was replaced by plain SQLite to reduce dependencies.
