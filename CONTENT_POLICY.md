# Content and grammatical verification policy

- Source-derived Sanskrit and Kannada material is the primary basis for lessons.
- English prose is newly authored, not a literal machine translation.
- Keep source distinctions between rule, condition, exception, optionality and derivational sequence.
- Do not silently repair disputed or inconsistent source examples; flag them for scholarly review.
- Do not use prohibited honorifics or reproduce them in UI, content, metadata, or credits.
- Do not show source Word document titles or filenames in the app.
- Engine outputs require an explicitly verified rule record and satisfied conditions.
- Display all verified vikalpa outcomes for the applicable context.
- Reverse analysis must preserve multiple plausible analyses and must not assert uniqueness without evidence.
- Unsupported input must return a clear not-feasible / not-yet-verified message, never an invented form.
